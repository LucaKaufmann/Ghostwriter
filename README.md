# Ghostwriter

Ghostwriter is a cross-platform reading workflow for turning feeds, newsletters, and media into digestible reading and listening sessions.

- **Ghostwriter** is the self-hosted backend and web UI.
- **Epilogue** is the mobile client family for reading and syncing digests.
- **Reader integrations** let you move generated EPUBs onto e-ink devices and other reading apps.

## Why Use It

- Build daily EPUB digests from RSS and Atom feeds.
- Choose between full-article extraction and AI-generated briefings.
- Sync digests and feed settings through your own Ghostwriter server.
- Generate optional AI-narrated podcast episodes from completed digests.
- Read comfortably on phones, tablets, and e-ink readers.
